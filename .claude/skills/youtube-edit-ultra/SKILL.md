description: Produce or resume a local sports montage from athlete, music style and duration using YouTube-Edit-Ultra, documented source permissions, reviewed action boundaries and measured final quality.

Read root `AGENTS.md` and `docs/AUTONOMOUS_WORKFLOW.md` in the YouTube-Edit-Ultra checkout. For shot schemas, preserved mixes or audio intent, read `docs/EDITORIAL_WORKFLOW.md`.

1. Inspect the current job, source permissions, reports and running processes. Run `ultra doctor`, then use `ueu create "<athlete> — <style> — <duration>"`. Resume with `ueu resume JOB`; inspect saved state with `ueu status JOB`. Keep legacy interfaces and the banner intact.
2. Let discovery/acquisition use the persistent catalogue, authorized URLs and available licensed music. Retain actual evidence. Public access is not permission; personal-use scope is not a publication grant. Commercial mode requires explicit recorded rights for music and footage and still does not guarantee monetization. Never bypass access restrictions.
3. Preserve decade/style requirements and music history. Do not substitute modern stock for historical R&B. Spotify supplies paginated metadata, never downloadable audio; explicit playlists remain binding. Keep credentials/callbacks private.
4. Inspect candidates and their complete boundaries. One-shot jobs require explicit reviewed provenance; motion or a model score cannot certify a combination, athlete identity or KO. If accessible evidence cannot establish safe boundaries, retain candidates and report `ACTION_REVIEW_REQUIRED` rather than inventing annotations. Continue independent discovery/review where possible without making the user coordinate the pipeline.
5. Preserve accepted audio/thumbnail assets on video revisions. Use reviewed cores/margins and available intro/outro context; action completeness takes priority over beats. The default music backend is portable FFmpeg/NumPy. Set `audio_intent` only from explicit editorial evidence: `auto`, `mute`, `ambience` or `featured`. Featured source audio receives bounded gain and a smooth music duck, not guessed speech recognition.
6. Respect estimates and disk-guard's normal 12 GB floor. No threshold override, inherited exception or deletion of prior work. Reuse checkpoints and caches after interruption. Only validated job-owned temporaries qualify for cleanup.
7. Read the final full-decode, timing/frame/format, true-peak, duplicate and action-integrity reports. Audio-only recovery must preserve video packets and be rechecked. Incomplete scans or unresolved flags block delivery. Report the actual local MP4, evidence paths and remaining review limits; technical success is not continuous human viewing or artistic acceptance.
8. Do not publish unless explicitly requested. Record verified work and limits in the project journal. Keep original Crawford/Canelo media and past finals unchanged. Distinguish real short fixture exports, simulated HTTP/Ollama/disk tests and actual historical-media checks.

Historical resumption references: `docs/LEGACY_EXECUTION_DIAGNOSIS.md` and `docs/PRODUCTION_VALIDATION.md`. Import old manifests as evidence; do not blindly rerun historical scripts. Analysis-only music records are not proof of use. No successful past export overrides current rights, review or storage blockers.
